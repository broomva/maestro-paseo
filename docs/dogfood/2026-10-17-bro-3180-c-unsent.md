# Dogfood receipt: an answer the link swallowed is kept as Not sent, and sent again by a tap (BRO-3180 C, #89)

Date: 2026-10-17. Ticket: BRO-3180, part C. Deployed as bbec6bc (live checkout `~/broomva/apps/maestro-paseo`, `paseo plugin reload maestro`).

## Surface, and what it is not

| Surface | What it was | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **The deployed build, at phone size** | The running Paseo.app main process over its Node inspector (127.0.0.1:9229), as in the part-A and part-B receipts. A hidden 402×874 `BrowserWindow` with the app's preload opened the live plugin on the live daemon. A "reconnect" was the window destroyed and a new one opened: a fresh evaluation of the bundle, as Paseo does when the link comes back. | The real plugin, daemon and ledger path. **Not the iOS app and not a real network drop.** The fault was injected in the page, below. |

**How the link was "dropped".** `WebSocket.prototype.send` in the page was wrapped so that a frame carrying a `plugin.rpc.invoke.request` for `ask.answer` is discarded. All other frames passed. The answer call therefore got no reply, and Paseo's client gave up after its own timeout: `Timeout waiting for message (60000ms)`. A fresh window carries no such wrapper.

**Writes:** one **fixture** ledger only, `.control/asks/bro-3180-c-dogfood-fixture.yaml` (ask C1). It was untracked in a throwaway workspace worktree, as in the BRO-3146 receipt. It was answered in place and deleted afterwards. The phone keys in Paseo.app's storage were cleared, and the hidden window was destroyed. **No owner ask was answered.**

## Rows (spec "Acceptance, PR C")

| Row | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A send that gets no reply is kept | **pass** | One `ask.answer` frame was dropped. After the 60 s client timeout, the card read "Not confirmed: the link dropped before your Mac answered, so this may not have been sent". Its verbs became **Skip / Send again**. `1-not-sent-after-dropped-send.png` |
| After a reconnect it is still there, resolved from fresh reads | **pass** | A fresh window reopened the card on C1 ("Ask · 1 of 1"). With `verbs.list` and the asks read after the send, it read "Not sent: your Mac has no answer to this ask". The device record: `{hash: d28e6c88ebeb335e, key: ask:[…,"bro-3180-c-dogfood-fixture.yaml",null,"C1"], error: "Timeout waiting for message (60000ms)"}`. Nothing was sent by coming back. `2-after-reconnect-not-sent.png` |
| The ask changed meanwhile: **Send again refuses** | **pass** | C1's headline was edited, which changes its hash, and the window reconnected. The card read "Not sent, and this ask changed after you answered. Read it again before you send." `3-changed-ask.png`. One tap on **Send again** sent nothing: the record moved onto the new hash `7ee945d51f1ce6e7`, and **the ledger had no answer written**. `4-first-tap-refused.png` |
| A second, deliberate tap answers the revision on screen | **pass** | The second tap gave "Answered: Lane A · applies in 7 s" with **Undo**, and the record cleared. After the window closed, the ledger read `answer_option: "lane-a"`, `answered_at: "2026-10-17T16:56:08Z"`, `status: "resolved"`. `5-second-tap-sent-undo.png` |

The scripts are beside the shots. `c-open.js` opens the window and lists the series; `c-fail.js` installs the fault and taps Answer; `c-return.js` reconnects and reads the card and the store; `c-tap.js` taps Send again; `cdp-eval.mjs` is the driver.

## What only the phone can show

- A real link drop on iOS. There Paseo unmounts Maestro, so a failed send's record must already be on the device (`Settings`). The record is written with `flushDrafts` at failure time.
- Answering while actually offline is not possible inside a plugin today. It needs getpaseo/paseo#7247.
