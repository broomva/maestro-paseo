**A trap in the surface, fixed in the script.** `Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled` sent *before* `loadURL` never
answers (the page has no target yet), and the first run hung on it. `b-live.js` now sends it after the load, with a
5 s cap, and destroys hidden windows left over from an earlier run.

Scripts beside the shots: `b-live.js` (leave and come back), `b-live-reload.js` (after a plugin reload), and `cdp-eval.mjs` (the driver).
